A LinkedIn carousel is a document-style post made of multiple slides that users swipe through in the LinkedIn feed. Each slide can contain text, images, charts, or a mix of all three.

Carousels are one of the most engaging content formats on LinkedIn because they encourage users to stop, swipe, and save.

## What is a LinkedIn carousel?

A LinkedIn carousel post is a PDF or image-based document that displays as a swipeable deck inside the LinkedIn feed. Viewers click or swipe to move from one slide to the next.

Carousels are commonly used to share:

- Step-by-step guides
- Lists and frameworks
- Data breakdowns
- Case studies
- Short lessons or tutorials

## Why LinkedIn carousels work

Carousels perform well because they combine three powerful things:

1. **Curiosity** — the first slide teases what is coming.
2. **Depth** — multiple slides let you cover a topic properly.
3. **Save value** — useful carousels get bookmarked and revisited.

LinkedIn also rewards carousels because the swipe action keeps users on the platform longer.

## How to create a LinkedIn carousel

There are three ways to create a carousel:

### 1. Native LinkedIn document post

Upload a PDF directly to LinkedIn. This is the simplest option but offers limited design control.

### 2. Design tools like Canva

Use templates to design each slide, then export as PDF or images.

### 3. AI carousel generators like BrandOps

Generate research, copy, and slide artwork from one topic, then review and publish.

## What makes a good LinkedIn carousel?

A strong carousel has:

- A clear hook on the first slide
- One idea per slide
- Simple, readable design
- A call to action on the last slide
- Professional visuals that match your brand

## FAQ

### Can I use a LinkedIn carousel on a company page?

Yes. LinkedIn document posts work on both personal profiles and company pages.

### How many slides should a LinkedIn carousel have?

Most successful carousels have 5 to 12 slides. Fewer than 5 feels thin. More than 12 can lose attention.

### Do carousels get more reach than text posts?

Generally yes. Carousels tend to get more engagement because the swipe action signals interest to LinkedIn's algorithm.

### What is the best file format for a LinkedIn carousel?

PDF is the most reliable format for LinkedIn document posts. Images can also work but are harder to edit after posting.
