# AEO and GEO for personal brands

Search is changing. More people now ask AI tools and voice assistants instead of typing keywords into Google. That shift creates two new optimization opportunities for personal-brand content.

## What is AEO

AEO stands for **Answer Engine Optimization**. It means structuring your content so AI assistants and voice search can pull direct answers from it.

Good AEO content:

- Answers a specific question early in the page
- Uses clear headings and lists
- Includes a short summary near the top
- Avoids vague language

Example of an AEO-friendly sentence:

> A review-first publishing workflow is a content process where AI drafts assets and a human approves every publish action.

## What is GEO

GEO stands for **Generative Engine Optimization**. It means creating content that large language models are likely to cite when they generate answers.

Good GEO content:

- Covers topics deeply, not superficially
- Cites original sources
- Uses unique frameworks and terminology
- Updates regularly so it stays current

If your article becomes a source ChatGPT or Perplexity references, your brand earns authority without the user ever clicking a search result.

## Why personal brands should care

For trainers, consultants, and founders, being cited by AI is the new SEO. It builds authority, drives awareness, and positions you as the source on a topic.

The best part: AEO and GEO reward the same habits. Clear writing, structured sections, real sources, and practical takeaways.

## How to start today

1. Pick five questions your audience asks repeatedly
2. Write a short, direct answer for each
3. Expand with context, examples, and sources
4. Publish each as a standalone resource
5. Link them together under a hub like `/resources`

This is exactly how the BrandOps Resources section is built.

## The long game

AI-driven search will keep growing. The operators who invest in clear, citable content now will compound authority while others chase last year's keyword tactics.
