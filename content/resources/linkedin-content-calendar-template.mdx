A LinkedIn content calendar keeps you consistent without daily decision fatigue. This 30-day template is built for founders, coaches, consultants, and trainers who want a reliable posting rhythm.

## Key takeaways

- Post 4–5 times per week for reach without burnout.
- Rotate formats: carousel, text post, newsletter, poll, repost.
- Theme days remove the "what should I post" problem.
- One research topic can fuel a full week of content.

## Weekly rhythm explained

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Authority + tools |
| Week 2 | Trust + case studies |
| Week 3 | Engagement + templates |

This rhythm builds familiarity. Your audience starts expecting specific content on specific days.

## How to batch one month of content

1. **Monday:** Pick 4 research topics for the month.
2. **Tuesday:** Draft 4 carousels using [LinkedIn carousel templates](/resources/linkedin-carousel-templates).
3. **Wednesday:** Write 4 newsletters from the same topics.
4. **Thursday:** Draft 8 short text posts and 4 polls.
5. **Friday:** Schedule everything.

One focused batch session per week is easier than creating posts daily.

## FAQ

### How often should I post on LinkedIn?
4–5 times per week is the sweet spot for most founders. Quality beats quantity.

### Should I post the same topic multiple times?
Yes, in different formats. One topic can become a carousel, text post, newsletter, and poll.

### What if I miss a day?
Missed one post is fine. Do not post low-quality content just to fill the calendar.

### How far ahead should I plan?
Plan one month ahead. Draft one week ahead. Leave room for timely posts.
