Use these LinkedIn carousel templates when you want engagement without staring at a blank page. Each framework includes a proven hook, slide structure, and real example.

## Key takeaways

- Seven proven carousel frameworks work across every industry.
- Each template has a hook-first slide and a clear final slide CTA.
- The best carousels teach one idea, not ten.
- BrandOps can turn your research topic into a draft of any of these templates.

---

## Template 1: The List Carousel

**Best for:** Quick tips, tools, resources, mistakes.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: "7 tools that saved me 10 hours this month" |
| 2–7 | One tool per slide with one-line benefit |
| 8 | CTA: "Which one will you try first?" |

Why it works: lists are easy to scan and easy to save.

---

## Template 2: The Step-by-Step Process

**Best for:** How-to content, tutorials, workflows.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: "How I write a LinkedIn post in 12 minutes" |
| 2–6 | One step per slide with a simple visual |
| 7 | CTA: "Save this for your next post" |

Why it works: process carousels get shared because they promise a repeatable result.

---

## Template 3: The Mistakes Framework

**Best for:** Authority building, trust, relatable content.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: "5 mistakes I made when starting on LinkedIn" |
| 2–6 | One mistake + what to do instead |
| 7 | CTA: "What mistake would you add?" |

Why it works: people engage with vulnerability and corrected mistakes.

---

## Template 4: The Comparison Slide

**Best for:** Product reviews, tool comparisons, before/after.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: "Manual vs AI-powered content creation" |
| 2 | Old way: time, cost, inconsistency |
| 3 | New way: speed, citations, review-first control |
| 4 | CTA: "See how it works" |

Why it works: contrast makes value obvious.

---

## Template 5: The Story Arc

**Best for:** Founder stories, case studies, lessons learned.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: "I lost $12K before I learned this" |
| 2 | Setup: the problem |
| 3–5 | The journey and turning point |
| 6 | The lesson |
| 7 | CTA: "Follow for more founder lessons" |

Why it works: stories create emotional connection and saves.

---

## Template 6: The Data / Research Breakdown

**Best for:** Original research, cited insights, trend analysis.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: "3 data points that changed how I think about LinkedIn" |
| 2–4 | One stat per slide with source |
| 5 | Your interpretation |
| 6 | CTA: "Download the full report" |

Why it works: data builds authority and gets referenced by others.

---

## Template 7: The Myth-Buster

**Best for:** Controversial takes, common misconceptions.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: "5 LinkedIn myths that are killing your reach" |
| 2–6 | Myth vs reality |
| 7 | CTA: "What myth surprised you?" |

Why it works: contradiction triggers comments and shares.

---

## How to choose the right template

| Goal | Best template |
|---|---|
| Get saves | List or process |
| Build trust | Mistakes or story |
| Start a debate | Myth-buster |
| Show expertise | Data breakdown |
| Drive clicks | Comparison |

## FAQ

### Which carousel template gets the most saves?
The list and step-by-step templates get the most saves because they are easy to reference later.

### How many slides should a LinkedIn carousel have?
5–12 slides is the sweet spot. Fewer than 5 feels thin. More than 12 hurts completion rate.

### Can I use the same template every week?
Yes, but rotate the framework so your audience does not tune out. Use 3–4 templates on a loop.

### Do I need design skills?
