# AI Newsletter Cheat Sheet

Use this cheat sheet to write a structured, cited newsletter from one research topic.

## Newsletter anatomy

| Section | Purpose | Word target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject line | Get the open | 6–10 words |
| Preview text | Support the subject | 8–12 words |
| Opening | State the change | 30–50 words |
| Context | Explain what drove it | 80–120 words |
| Implication | What it means for the reader | 80–120 words |
| Action | One concrete step this week | 40–60 words |
| Close | Tie back to your point of view | 20–40 words |

## Subject-line formulas

| Formula | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Number + outcome | 3 AI workflows that save 5 hours a week |
| Question + consequence | Is your LinkedIn carousel missing this slide? |
| How to + result | How to turn one story into two posts |
| Timely + angle | What AWS's free-tier change means for bootstrapped teams |

## Citation rules

- Link every factual claim to a source.
- Use inline links, not footnotes, for readability.
- Cite 4–7 sources per newsletter.
- Always verify the source date is recent.
- Never cite AI output as a primary source.

## Tone rules

- Write to one reader, not a crowd.
- Use "you" more than "we."
- One em dash per email maximum.
- Avoid filler openers like "I hope this finds you well."

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Subject line is under 10 words.
- [ ] Every claim links to a source.
- [ ] Lead visual is on-brand and text-free.
- [ ] Call to action is a single action.
- [ ] Sender name matches your personal brand.
- [ ] Test email sent and links clicked.
- [ ] Recipient list is correct.

## Quick template

**Subject:** [Specific change] and what [audience] should do now

**Opening:** [Change] happened this week.

**Context:** [Driver] caused it. [Source]

**Implication:** For [audience], this means [outcome].

**Action:** This week, do [one specific thing].

**Close:** This is the kind of story [your brand] tracks every week.

---

Use this template every time you draft a newsletter. It keeps your structure consistent and your claims defensible.
