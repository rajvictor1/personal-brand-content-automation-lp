## 1. Why Query Fan-Out Changes Our Strategy

Google AI Mode / AI Overviews do not answer from one page. They break a query into 6–15 sub-queries and combine answers from multiple sources.

For BrandOps, this means we need a **topic cluster**, not isolated articles.
