## 3. Target answer formats for AEO

Each new and existing article should include at least one of these answer-friendly formats:

### 3.1 Definition block
Used in glossary/intro content.

> **Review-first publishing** is a workflow where AI generates drafts, but a human approves every asset before any publish or send action.

### 3.2 Step-by-step list
Used in workflow guides.

> 1. Pick one timely topic.
> 2. Research for 15 minutes.
> 3. Generate a carousel and newsletter draft.
> 4. Review every slide and paragraph.
> 5. Publish only after approval.

### 3.3 Comparison table
Used in tool/buying guides. Highly citable by AI engines.

| Tool | Carousel | Newsletter | Review gate | Best for |
|------|----------|------------|-------------|----------|
| BrandOps | Yes | Yes | Yes | Solo operators who want control |
| Copy.ai | No | Yes | No | Short-form copy |
| Canva | Yes | No | No | Visual design |

### 3.4 FAQ section
Use H3 questions directly. Match "People also ask" patterns.

### 3.5 Key Takeaways box
Summarize the article in 4–6 bullets at the top. AI engines love this format.
