# Demo Script: AI Findability Scorecard for Personal Brands

**Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds

**Opening hook (15s):**

"Most personal brand websites look great to humans but are invisible to AI search. I built a free scorecard that checks if ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews can actually find and cite you."

**Screen share and walkthrough:**

1. **Show the landing page (15s)**
   - Open https://www.brandops.site/geo-scorecard.
   - Read the headline: 'AI Findability Scorecard for Personal Brands.'
   - Point to the promise: 'One URL. One minute. A clear picture of how AI search engines see your site.'

2. **Submit a real URL (20s)**
   - Enter a website URL from the chat or a known personal brand site.
   - Enter a real-looking email.
   - Check the consent box.
   - Click 'Get my free AI Findability score.'

3. **Show the loading state (10s)**
   - 'Analyzing your site…'
   - Mention it checks 6 categories in real time.

4. **Reveal the score (30s)**
   - Show the overall score out of 100.
   - Show the headline: Highly AI-findable / AI-findable with gaps / Weak AI findability / Hard to find by AI.
   - Walk through the 6 category cards one by one.

5. **Highlight quick wins (30s)**
   - Read the top 3 quick wins aloud.
   - Explain that each one can be done in under an hour.

6. **Download the PDF (20s)**
   - Click 'Download PDF report.'
   - Show the first page of the PDF.
   - Mention it is personalized to their URL and score.

7. **Close with CTA and distribution (30s)**
   - 'This is the lead magnet. It captures an email, proves value in 60 seconds, and gives them a reason to come back to BrandOps.'
   - 'Next, I post the link on LinkedIn with a real score screenshot and invite people to drop their score in the comments.'

**Closing line:**

"Lead magnets only work if the value is instant. This one is."

**Fallback if the site fails to fetch:**

- Say: "Some sites block third-party crawlers. That is itself a finding. The scorecard reports that as a critical issue and recommends allowing AI crawlers in robots.txt."
- Use https://www.brandops.site as a reliable demo URL.
